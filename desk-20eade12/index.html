Your bookings save to the same Google Sheet as your requests. It needs the new version of the script:

  1. Open your Fieldwork inbox sheet, then Extensions → Apps Script.
  2. Select all the old code, paste the new script over it, and tap Save.
  3. Deploy → Manage deployments, tap the pencil, set Version to New version, then Deploy.

The link stays the same. Pull down here to refresh when you’re done.
